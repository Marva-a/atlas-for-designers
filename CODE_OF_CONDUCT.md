# Code of conduct

Atlas for Designers is a place to learn and to teach. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else in the project, agrees to:

- **Be respectful and kind.** Critique work, never people. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- **Be welcoming to beginners.** Many learners and contributors are new to the domain they're writing about or studying. Explain; don't gatekeep.
- **Give useful feedback.** Say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it, with a source where you can.
- **Be honest.** Disclose AI assistance, credit others' work, and say so when you aren't sure.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal information, and never ask learners for their data.

Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, sustained disruption, and publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@Marva-a](https://github.com/Marva-a)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and block participants, that break this code.
